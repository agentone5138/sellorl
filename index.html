FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast can you actually close?+
Most closings happen within 10–60 days of accepting an offer, through a licensed Florida title company. If you need more time, we can work around your schedule instead.
Do I need to make any repairs or clean the house out first?+
No. We buy properties in as-is condition, including homes with deferred maintenance, storm damage, or belongings left behind.
How is my cash offer calculated?+
We look at the property's condition, location, and recent comparable sales nearby, then subtract estimated repair and holding costs to arrive at a fair, all-cash number.
Are there any fees, commissions, or closing costs?+
None on your end. There are no agent commissions and no closing costs — the offer you accept is what you walk away with.
What if the property is in probate?+
We regularly work with executors and heirs on probate properties, and can coordinate directly with your attorney or the estate's title company.
What if I still have tenants living there?+
That's fine — we buy occupied rental properties as well as vacant ones, and can work through lease or tenant transitions as part of closing.
Am I obligated to accept the offer?+
Never. Requesting an offer is free and comes with no obligation to sell.
What areas around Orlando do you buy in?+
We buy throughout Orange, Seminole, Osceola, Lake, and Volusia counties — including Orlando, Winter Park, Sanford, Kissimmee, Clermont, and Daytona Beach.
